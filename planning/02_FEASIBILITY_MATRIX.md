## Resolved Challenges

The brief's three mandatory predictive capabilities become two prioritized experiments (delivery and aggregate forecasts) plus a conditional CX extension. A failed baseline comparison is an acceptable capstone finding. Models are not separate services by default. RFM clustering and duration regression do not justify additional production components. “Sales demand” is renamed **recorded order volume** because capture coverage and underlying demand are unobserved.

## Assumptions / Evidence / Open Questions

Evidence: all nine files fully scanned, with exact schema, hashes and cohort counts in the audit. Assumptions: static reference attributes and promised date can be attached at placement, explicitly disclosed. Open questions: team capacity, rubric requirements for model count, alert review capacity and intended noncommercial audience. These are owner choices; no additional model is approved merely to satisfy a count.
